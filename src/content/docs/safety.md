AgOpenGPS is a community project. Farmers, hobbyists and developers build it in
their spare time and share it for free. It is **not a certified product**. It
has not been tested or approved to the safety standards that factory autosteer
systems are built to, and no one checks how each system is built or installed.

**If you build or use an AgOpenGPS system, you do so at your own risk, and you
are responsible for it.**

## You stay in control

- Autosteer only helps you steer. It does not drive the machine.
- Stay in the seat, watch where you're going, and be ready to take over at any
  moment, especially near people, animals, field edges, ditches, power line
  poles and other obstacles.
- Never leave a machine unattended while autosteer is on.

## Never on public roads

- Turn autosteer off before leaving the field, and make sure it cannot switch on
  by accident while you're on the road.
- Changing a vehicle's steering system can affect whether it is road-legal and
  whether your insurance covers it. Check the rules in your country before you
  install anything.

## Before every use

- Test that the steering disengages when it should: when you turn the steering
  wheel (detected by an encoder, pressure sensor or current sensor, if fitted)
  and when you use the steer switch.
- Check that you can always cut power to the steering motor or valve straight
  away.
- Do these checks again after every change to wiring, firmware or settings.
  Firmware or configuration changes can switch off safety functions without you
  noticing.

## Hydraulics and electrics

- Hydraulic systems hold high pressure even with the engine off. Release the
  pressure before working on them, and never check for leaks with your hand.
- Fuse every power connection, and use wire and connectors rated for the current
  and for outdoor conditions.

## About this documentation

- These pages are written by volunteers. They may be incomplete, out of date or
  wrong for your machine. Check the information against your machine's manuals,
  and ask on the [forum](https://discourse.agopengps.com) if you're unsure.
